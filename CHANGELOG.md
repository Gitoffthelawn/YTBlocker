## v1.8.1 2026-08-27

### Fixed

- Added the multilingual resources that were inadvertently omitted from v1.8.0

## v1.8.0 2026-08-27

### Added

- Added block-list registration buttons to the menu for the currently playing video. This does not block playback immediately; it only prevents the video from appearing in recommendation cards. After registering a video, navigating to another video and then returning with the browser's Back button does not interrupt playback. Videos can still be played when opened from a known URL.
